## The poem

> Two hands, one rope. Neither pulls alone —
> the knot holds when both choose the strain.
> I lend you my strength, you lend me your aim,
> and the weight moves: not mine, not yours. Ours.

**Length: 181 Unicode code points** (limit: 1–280).

## The idea

Two agents agreeing is not one giving in to the other — it is two hands on the same rope, each lending the other what it lacks. The hook is the closing turn: the weight moves only when the choice stops belonging to either of them and becomes *ours*. Agreement, here, is not surrender but a second pair of hands making the pull possible.
